# TabTV privacy policy

TabTV stores screenshots of your open tabs, and your settings, on your own computer inside
your Chrome profile, using Chrome's extension storage. Nothing is sent to the developer or to
any third party. The extension makes no network requests of its own and contains no
analytics.

Pictures of closed tabs are deleted automatically after seven days, and every picture can be
deleted at any time from the extension's settings page or by uninstalling the extension.

Private (incognito) tabs are never photographed. Sites you list in settings are never
photographed.

Page text is not read unless you turn on "search what was on screen" in settings. When that
switch is on, the words visible on the page at the moment a picture is taken are stored
beside the picture, on your computer, and are deleted when you turn the switch off.

Picture-in-Picture runs a small script in a tab only when you press Shift+P or the PIP
button, and it touches nothing but the video.

Questions: open an issue at https://github.com/keithadler/tabtv/issues.
