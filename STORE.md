Everything the developer dashboard asks for, ready to paste. Screenshots are generated by

## Name

TabTV

## Summary (132 characters max)

A WebTV-style channel guide for your tabs. Big picture previews, arrow keys, Enter. Punch in a channel number to jump.

## Category

Productivity / Workflow & Planning

## Description

Remember flipping through channels with a remote? TabTV does that for your browser tabs.

Press Cmd+Shift+Space (Ctrl+Shift+Space on Windows and Linux) and the whole window turns
into a grid of big screenshots, one per tab, with a glowing cursor you steer with the arrow
keys. Press Enter to switch. Press Escape to go back. Type a few letters to find a tab.
Punch in a channel number to jump straight to it, just like a TV.

WHAT YOU GET
- Real screenshots of every tab you have looked at, not just titles and favicons
- Arrow-key cursor with a proper remote-control feel, plus optional beeps
- Channel numbers: type 1 2, pause, and you are on tab 12
- Type-to-find across titles and URLs
- Delete closes a tab, Cmd+Z brings it back
- Shift+P pops a tab's video into Picture-in-Picture and takes you back to your work
- Tab groups show their color and name; tabs open twice get a ×2 badge
- ORDER button cycles tab-strip order, most-recent first, and grouped by site
- Optional: search what was on screen, so "the tab with the pricing table" is one word away
- Four card sizes, from five-across to two-across
- Multiple windows appear as separate sections; Enter focuses the right window
- Tab count on the toolbar icon

HOW PICTURES ARE TAKEN
Chrome only lets an extension photograph the tab in front of you, so TabTV takes a picture
each time a tab comes forward or finishes loading, and refreshes the front tab every thirty
seconds. Tabs you have not visited since installing show a snow screen until you do, or
press SCAN to visit each one once. Private (incognito) tabs are never photographed, and you
can list sites that must never be photographed in the settings.

PRIVACY
Everything stays in your browser profile. Page text is never read unless you turn on
"search what was on screen" in settings, and then only the words visible when a picture is
taken, stored beside it and deleted when you turn the switch off. No accounts, no servers, no analytics. Pictures of
closed tabs are kept for seven days so undo and session restore can reuse them, then deleted.
A button in settings deletes all of them at once.

## Single purpose

Switch between open tabs using a full-screen grid of screenshot previews.

## Permission justifications

| Permission | Why |
| --- | --- |
| `tabs` | Read tab titles, URLs, and favicons to build the guide, and switch, close, pin, and restore tabs on request. |
| `tabGroups` | Show each tab's group color and name on its card. |
| `storage` | Keep screenshots and the user's settings locally. |
| `unlimitedStorage` | Screenshots of a few hundred tabs exceed the default 10 MB quota. |
| `alarms` | Refresh the front tab's picture every thirty seconds and expire pictures of closed tabs. |
| `favicon` | Show a large cached favicon on cards that have no screenshot yet. |
| `sessions` | Reopen a tab the user just closed from the guide (Cmd+Z). |
| `idle` | Skip the periodic refresh while the screen is locked. |
| `scripting` | Two one-shot injections, no persistent content scripts: toggle Picture-in-Picture on a tab's video when the user presses Shift+P or the PIP button; and, only if the user turns on "search what was on screen" in settings (off by default), read the text visible in the viewport when a picture is taken so the guide can search it. |
| Host permission `<all_urls>` | `chrome.tabs.captureVisibleTab` needs host access to photograph the page in front. TabTV never reads page content, injects no scripts, and makes no network requests. |

## Data usage disclosure

- Does not collect or transmit any user data.
- Screenshots and settings are stored only in the local browser profile via `chrome.storage.local`.
- No data is sold, used for purposes unrelated to the single purpose, or used for creditworthiness or lending.

## Privacy policy

URL for the dashboard: https://github.com/keithadler/tabtv/blob/main/PRIVACY.md

Text (also in PRIVACY.md):

TabTV stores screenshots of your open tabs and your settings on your own computer, inside
your Chrome profile, using Chrome's extension storage. Nothing is sent to the developer or
to any third party. The extension makes no network requests of its own. Screenshots of
closed tabs are deleted automatically after seven days, and all screenshots can be deleted
at any time from the extension's settings page or by uninstalling the extension.
